PRACTICE SCRIPT (2-3 sentences each, excited professor): SONIA (GLP-1 Adherence Agent, 0.96): Sonia partnered with a real health startup. Patients on weight-loss drugs like Ozempic stop logging their doses, so the care team has no idea who is still on track. Her agent messages you at your dosing time, you answer like a human, "took it with lunch," and it understands, logs it, and flags the patients who are slipping. JACKIE (Echo Culture Pulse, 1.0): Jackie built a culture radar for small companies. Employees answer a short anonymous survey every month, and her agent reads all of it, finds the themes, and tells leadership what is improving and what is quietly getting worse. If someone sounds burned out or about to quit, it raises a flag so it never gets buried. DANIEL (Daily Negotiation Practice, 0.85): Daniel decided negotiation is a sport, so you should train every day. His agent sends you one short scenario a day, a salary discussion, a vendor deal, and the other side has its own secret goals, so you actually have to negotiate. Afterwards it debriefs you: what worked, and the one thing to do better tomorrow. SOHAM (Inventory Cash Coach, 0.91): Soham took a classic operations problem, a retailer with thousands of products and limited cash. His agent goes item by item and recommends restock, hold, or clear out, with quantities, timing, and the reasoning, and a human planner approves. It is like having an AI analyst on every single product. MATT (Factory Feasibility Check, 0.79): Matt works on food manufacturing, where the question is always "can this line actually make this new product?" His agent reads the plant documents, the equipment specs, the certifications, and comes back with a risk score and what could go wrong. Weeks of engineering back-and-forth become a first answer in minutes. VINCENT (Larry, 1.0): Vincent built Larry, a career coach for incoming students. Before you even arrive, it reads your CV, interviews you about your goals, and builds you a plan: your gaps, and exactly which career-center workshops to attend. And the career center gets a dashboard of what the whole cohort actually wants. RENEE (First Look AI, 0.88): Renee automated the most tedious job in venture capital, the first pass on a screening list. Her agent reads each startup's website, fills the analyst template, and writes the partner-ready summary. And it is not allowed to guess: if the information is not on the website, it writes "not available." RICHA (ASF Support Bot, 1.0): Richa built a support agent for a nonprofit that helps six hundred scholarship students in India, with a staff of five. It does the monthly check-ins, understands replies, including voice notes in Hindi, and escalates the concerning ones to a human. It cut the routine load of that tiny team by more than half. CONNOR (PropScroll Homebuyer Guide, 0.91): Connor took on the scariest purchase of your life, your first home. His guide walks you stage by stage and tells you in plain language what a "comfortable" versus a "stretch" budget means for you. And there is no commission behind the advice. Alternates (all video + top grades): Donovan (stay-in-touch follow-up coach, 0.94), Will (on-screen ERP guide, 0.94), MaryAlice (therapist action filter, 0.91), Shamsul (pharma trial tracker, 0.92), Paul (lease renewal helper, 1.0), Andrea (used-car co-pilot, 0.88). Why an MBA right now is the perfect place to do this. One: you have time to think, you are not in the middle of work. Two: AI rewards experience, and you already have it: you have seen issues, what works, what does not. Add a sprinkle of AI and rethink how things are done.
