## Talk to Kai, the AI teaching assistant

**Kai** is the AI teaching assistant that runs every week of AIML 901, my AI course for Kellogg MBAs. What is embedded below is a real homework from that course, exactly as students got it. It is graded on effort, not on answers. Say "start the homework" to run it, or just ask Kai about the course.
