AI is moving faster than any technology most of us have seen. In four years it went from autocompleting sentences to answering questions, using tools, doing real work on its own, and now remembering what it learns about you. In this Kellogg Preview Day masterclass, Sébastien Martin showed live what today’s AI tools can actually do, what Kellogg is doing with AI in the classroom and around it, and why this is a great moment to start an MBA, with practical advice to get started yourself.
