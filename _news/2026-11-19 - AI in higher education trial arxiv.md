New paper on arXiv: ["Instructor-designed AI improves learning in a randomized trial across 19 universities"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19099), with Jun Li, Rob Bray, Nils Van den Steen, Andrew Wu and colleagues at 19 universities. In a pre-registered trial with 7,200 students over three semesters, AI tools that instructors designed for their own classes improved student learning, measured with proctored, in-person exams.
