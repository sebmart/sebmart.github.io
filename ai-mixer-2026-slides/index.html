Community brainstorming

Brainstorming time

Let's fill these whiteboards together!
  • Talk with your table: students, staff and faculty you may not know yet.
  • Three questions, about 10 minutes each.
  • One idea per sticky note, as many as you can. There are no bad ideas!
  • We'll share the outcome with the whole community, so we can move faster together.
Question 1 of 3

Where are we now?

So much is happening with AI across Kellogg, but it often stays within one group.
Great work can go unnoticed, even by the school. Let's put it all on the table.
What's happening with AI around you that others should know about? What has changed recently, and how do you feel about it?
Question 2 of 3

Where should we be now?

This is your chance to suggest what would help most today.
Not everything can happen at once, so what would make the biggest difference?
What do you wish existed at Kellogg right now? What would unlock you, your team or your classmates?
Think outside the box, and across boundaries.
Question 3 of 3

Where do we want to go, together?

AI is moving fast, so beyond today's needs, we also need to look further ahead.
Imagine you're in Kellogg's shoes.
What would you prioritize? How should we handle this transition so that it benefits our whole community?

Thank you!

Thank you for helping shape Kellogg's AI future. We'll gather every sticky note and share what comes out of it with the whole community.
